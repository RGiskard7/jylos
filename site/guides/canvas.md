# Canvas

The Canvas editor opens and edits Obsidian-compatible `.canvas` files on an infinite, pan/zoom surface.

## What you can do

- Create, move, resize and colour text, link and group nodes.
- Connect and delete edges, with arrowheads.
- Create new canvases.
- Select several cards and move, delete, copy and paste them together.

## Controls

- **Pan:** mouse wheel or two fingers on the trackpad (horizontally with Shift). Also by dragging the background with the middle button, or with the left one while holding Space.
- **Zoom:** Cmd/Ctrl + wheel, pinch on the trackpad, or the toolbar buttons.
- **Select:** click a card; Shift+click or Cmd/Ctrl+click adds or removes cards; dragging on the background draws a box that selects the cards it touches; Cmd/Ctrl+A selects them all.
- **Move:** drag a selected card and the whole selection moves. Cards snap to the grid and line up with the edges and centres of the others; both are switched on or off from the settings button in the toolbar and are remembered.
- **Copy, cut and paste:** Cmd/Ctrl+C, X and V, with the edges between the copied cards. They can be pasted into another canvas.
- **Delete:** Delete or Backspace deletes the selection.

## Safety

Saves round-trip safely: unknown fields are preserved, so a canvas produced or edited by another Obsidian-compatible tool stays intact.
